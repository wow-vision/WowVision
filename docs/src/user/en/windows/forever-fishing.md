# Forever Fishing

Available on WoW: Forever, with the third-party addon Forever Fishing. Without it, nothing on this page exists. Nothing of it is changed.

Forever Fishing's small window is drawn for the eyes and the mouse. WowVision says when it opens or closes and the messages it shows, and gives it a screen.

## Keys and menu

- Start Fishing, in the WowVision menu (Shift+F1), does what /fish does: it opens the window, and Forever Fishing equips the fishing gear. If the window is already open, WowVision says so and does not close it.
- Open the fishing window screen (Shift+F7): the information on the window (status, rod, gear, fishing key, skill, gold per hour, cast countdown) and the actions: start fishing, put on the fishing gear or your normal gear back, close the window and get your gear back.
- /wv fishing opens the screen; start, close, read, gold and messages on or off do the rest.

Say the Fishing Window's Messages (on) says the opening and closing of the window, the gear results and the warnings.

## Gold per hour

WowVision works it out from what the session caught: with the auction hints (Life on Azeroth Market), the auction house price after its cut when it beats the vendor's by 10 percent and the item is not bound, else the vendor's; without them, the vendor's price, and it says so. A figure is given after a minute of fishing.

In combat Forever Fishing does not change gear.
