### All Versions
* Pending windows: Escape on a popup that waits for an answer and ignores Escape in the game (a group invitation, a summon, death, a resurrection, the invitation that asks for your role, the ready check) now puts it aside: "Pending" is said, WowVision's window over it closes and your keys work in the game again. Nothing is answered, and the popup stays on screen. While something waits, the WowVision menu starts with Pending; Enter on an entry brings the popup back to the front. An entry goes by itself when the popup is answered, cancelled or runs out. The Close All Windows key (Shift+Escape) puts them aside too. Popups that take Escape in the game, such as deleting an item, keep the game's Escape. The Pending Windows setting (UI menu) chooses Escape and the Close All Windows Key (the default), Only the Close All Windows Key, or Off (as before).
* Trade: new Accept Trade key (Alt+Ctrl+T) accepts the open trade without going to the trade window, also in combat. "Trade accepted" is said once the game reports it; otherwise the key says why nothing happened (no trade open, already accepted and waiting for the other player, a popup about an enchant to answer first). When the other player changes the offer, that is said.
* Trade and mail: when the game asks for its security confirmation (a changed offer after you accepted, or mail with money or items to a stranger), WowVision says so. That dialog cannot be reached by addons or the keyboard: use the mouse, or cancel and start again.
* Monitors: new monitor type Resource. It follows one of your own resources and fires an alert per step: every point of a counted resource (combo points, holy power, chi and the like), or every 10 to 50 percent of a bar (mana, energy, rage and the like). Every step has its own alert with a sound and a speech switch, in the monitor's Steps submenu; the monitor sets the sound series, the speech format, the voice, rate and volume, and a minimum time between messages. On Retail and WoW: Forever the game hides mana and energy from addons, so the monitor offers only the resources it can read there.
* Monitors: new Speak Main Resource key (not bound by default) says the resource the game shows for you now, for example "energy 80%" or "combo points 3". It also works for the resources the game hides from addons.
* Translations: the name a copy gets ("Moonfire Copy 1") has its own text, CopyName, apart from the Copy menu entry, for languages that word the two differently.

### Modern
* Group health: Include Yourself, Watch Party, Watch Raid and the Raid Groups switches now take effect at once. Before, a change waited for someone to join or leave the group, or a reload.

### Forever
* WowNav: a new route starts at the entry point at the height you came from. WowVision remembers the height of the last waypoint of the network you walked to; the entry point selected for you is then the closest one at about that height (within 4 yards), not simply the closest one, so the road under a bridge or another floor of a building no longer wins just because it lies nearer. While you swim or dive, the nearest water point is still selected. The setting Guess the Start From the Last Height (Navigation menu, on by default) turns it off.
* WowNav: a route to a place whose nearest point lies in closed water (a pond without a way out) is now found at once. Before, the search covered the whole continent first; a corpse run in the Wetlands took 72 seconds.
