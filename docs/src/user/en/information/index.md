# Information on Demand

WowVision gives you ways to follow game information without looking at the screen:

- [Buffers](buffers.md) are lists you read whenever you want: your health, mana, gold, experience, bag space, the auras on your target, the messages of a chat tab, and much more. You step through them with Alt and the arrow keys, from anywhere in the game.
- [Monitors](monitors.md) watch something for you and tell you when it changes: a debuff falling off your target, a cooldown becoming ready, a proc. They speak or play a sound on their own.
- [Group Health Monitor](group-health.md) tells you which group members are hurt and how badly, by number and pitch (Retail and WoW: Forever).

A good rule of thumb: use a buffer for what you want to check, and a monitor for what you want to hear about.
