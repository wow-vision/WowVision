### All Versions
* Bags: the drag key on a bag slot places the bag you are holding, replacing the one in the slot, as Enter does. Before, it only picked up the bag that was in the slot.
* Enemy count: in a group, an enemy fighting your group counts at once, before you join the fight yourself; before, the game kept its threat list from WowVision until you were in the fight, often several seconds. It counts as long as no one outside your group has tapped it. A new number is now said after 0.3 seconds instead of half a second, as long as Sku waits for a pack.
