# Damage Meter (Details)

Available on WoW: Forever, with the third-party addon Details! Damage Meter. Without it, nothing on this page exists. On this version Details shows what the game's own damage meter delivers; WowVision asks the same meter, and asks Details only which fight and figures its first window shows. Nothing of Details is changed unless you reset its data.

## Keys

| Action | Default key | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Open the Details damage meter | Shift+F6 | Opens or closes the screen |
| Say my line in the Details damage meter | Alt+Ctrl+D | Your figure and rank, then the best when it is not you |
| Next measure of the damage meter | Alt+Ctrl+M | The next figures, said at once |
| Next fight of the damage meter | Alt+Ctrl+C | The next fight, said at once |

## The screen

Tab moves between the stops, Enter goes in, Escape goes up, then closes.

- Summary: the fight and figures with your line and rank, the best, the group.
- List: one line per player in rank order; Enter opens a player's spells, pets and, for damage done out of combat, targets.
- Display: the fight and the figures. Left and Right change one step and say the result; buttons show the current fight, all fights together or the last one kept.
- Actions: Follow the Details window, and Reset Details, which asks first.

Choosing a fight or figures yourself stops following Details until you check the box again.

## The command

/wv meter opens the screen; say, summary, top, status, segment, measure, follow, and reset (which asks, and erases only after /wv meter reset yes). The module's screen has the same buttons.

## Limits

In combat the game hides the meter's names and figures from addons: they are spoken, but no percent, total or targets until the fight ends. Resetting erases every fight and the total, never during a fight.
