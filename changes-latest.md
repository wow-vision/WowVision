### Modern
* Pet Health (Retail and WoW: Forever): WowVision now says your pet's health, which the Group Health Monitor does not watch. The new Speak Pet Health key (Alt+Shift+H) says it, for example "Pet 45 percent". With the new setting Announce Health Changes on (Pet Health, off by default), a change is also said by itself in a fight, at most every few seconds and only while WowVision has nothing else to say, and the pet's death at once.
