# Windows

## Guild invitation

When a player invites you into a guild, WowVision opens the invitation: who invites you, the guild's name, its achievement points where the game shows them, and a reputation warning when you would lose guild reputation. Tab reaches **Join Guild** and **Decline**.

Closing the invitation declines it, exactly like the game: **Escape** declines. The game also declines on its own after one minute.

On Classic Era and TBC the invitation is an ordinary popup and reads like every other popup.

## Guild and communities window

The guild key opens the guild and communities window (Retail and WoW: Forever). It has three parts:

- **The club list** on the left: your guild, your communities, invitations, and the finder entries. **Enter** selects one, **Backspace** opens the game's menu for it.
- **The page** in the middle, chosen with the tabs.
- **The tabs**, a row at the end: Chat, Roster, Guild Benefits, Guild Info, and Preferred Play where the game offers it.

The pages:

- **Chat**: the channel dropdown, the latest fifty messages (focus starts on the newest), the chat box (Enter in it sends), and the short member list.
- **Roster**: the column dropdown, the sort buttons, then every member with level, class, zone or last online, rank and note. **Enter** on a guild member opens the member card, **Backspace** the player menu.
- **Guild Info**: challenges, the message of the day and the guild information with their edit buttons, and the guild news.
- **Guild Benefits**: perks, rewards and the guild reputation.

The member card shows level, class, zone, rank, last online and the notes. **Enter** on a note you may edit opens the game's note box. A guild master or officer can change the rank in the rank dropdown, and use **Remove** and **Group Invite**.

**Guild Control** (on the roster, for the guild master and officers) opens rank management: a page dropdown, then the rank names with move up, move down and delete, the permission check boxes per rank, and the guild bank tab permissions.
