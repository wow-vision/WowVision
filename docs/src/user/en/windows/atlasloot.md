# AtlasLoot

Available on WoW: Forever, with the third-party addon AtlasLoot Forever (AtlasLootClassic) or AtlasLootContinued. Without one of them, nothing on this page exists. The two cannot be enabled together; WowVision works with either.

AtlasLoot's own window cannot be read with the keyboard. WowVision reads AtlasLoot's loot tables and shows them as a screen of its own. Nothing of AtlasLoot is changed.

## Opening it

| Way | What it does |
|---|---|
| Alt+L | Opens the loot browser, or closes it. Binding Open the loot browser (AtlasLoot) |
| /wv atlasloot | The same |
| /al | When AtlasLoot's window opens, the browser opens too, and closing it closes that window. The Open with AtlasLoot's window checkbox switches this off |

## Walking the tables

Enter goes down a level and Escape (or Back) goes up; at the top Escape closes. The levels are the loot categories, the dungeons and raids of a category (in AtlasLoot's groups, with their levels, and a Game version button when there are several), the bosses and other lists of an instance, then the loot. Where you were is kept until you close the game.

- All bosses together, above the first boss, gives the loot of every boss in one list, under each boss's name.
- Search by name, above the first dungeon, searches the whole category: type at least two letters; every word must be in the name, in any order, without regard to accents. Each result says where it drops. The lists are read a few at a time, so results fill in while you listen.

A loot line says the name, quality, slot and type, item level, the level it asks, the drop chance and AtlasLoot's note. Enter or Space reads the full tooltip, with WowVision's comparison and Pawn's summary.

## The filters

Tab moves between the list, the filters and the navigation.

- Only what my class can use (on): AtlasLoot's rules, except that armor follows the classic level rules (mail for hunters and shamans from level 40, plate for warriors and paladins from level 40).
- Only what my level allows.
- Only upgrades, judged by Sharpie's Gear Judge when it is installed, else Pawn, else item level; the Judge of upgrades setting chooses.
- Slot and Item type, offering only what the other filters leave.
- Sort: AtlasLoot's order, item level, biggest upgrade, slot or drop chance.

The summary always says how many items the filters hid. The checkboxes, the judge and the sort are settings, kept between sessions; slot, type and difficulty are not.

## Limits

Lists whose lines are not items (profession spells, achievements, reputations) are not read.
