### All Versions
* Bags: the drag key on a bag slot places the bag you are holding, replacing the one in the slot, as Enter does. Before, it only picked up the bag that was in the slot.
* Enemy count: in a group, an enemy fighting your group counts at once, before you join the fight yourself; before, the game kept its threat list from WowVision until you were in the fight, often several seconds. It counts while you, your pet or a group member is in combat and no one outside your group has tapped it, so while nobody in your group is fighting, an enemy that attacks a player outside your group before they hit it does not count. A new number is now said after 0.3 seconds instead of half a second, as long as Sku waits for a pack. A number that an interrupting alert (such as a target change) threw out of the speech queue before it was spoken is said again once when no newer number came within 5 seconds; before, the old number stood until the count changed, in long fights up to a minute.
* Recommended game settings: Apply Recommended Game Settings also turns on offscreen nameplates, so while enemy nameplates are shown, an enemy behind you or beside you gets its nameplate at once and the enemy count can say it without waiting for the camera to face it. Enemy nameplates themselves are not switched on by it.
