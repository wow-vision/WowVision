### All Versions
* Bags and bank: while the search box holds text, only the items that match are listed, as a plain list in slot order. Before, every slot stayed in the list and the ones that did not match said "Filtered", so a search still meant stepping through the whole bag. A bag without a match says "No results". Empty the box to get every slot back.
* Navigation: Announce Remaining Waypoints (Navigation menu) is now a dropdown instead of a checkbox: Always (the default, as before), The Last 10, The Last 5, The Last One, The Last 10 Percent, Every 10 or Never. The Last modes stay silent until that many waypoints are left, then say the count at every waypoint up to the end. Every 10 counts from the end and says every round ten and the waypoint before the last ("50 remaining", "40 remaining" ... "10 remaining", "1 remaining"). If you had the checkbox off, it is now Never.
* The Target Crowd Control alert and Aura buffers no longer depend on the game telling the addon that auras changed, which it does not do in some instances. The alert now looks at your target five times a second, and an Aura buffer counts its auras again every time you enter it or move in it. Before, crowd control landing on or ending on your current target could go unannounced there, and an Aura buffer could miss new auras and say "Gone" for ones that had ended.
* Group invitations: /wv accept and the Accept Group Invitation key now also accept when a group member suggests inviting someone or a player asks to join your group, also in combat. Before, they said "No group invitation" there, and the question could not be answered until the fight ended.
* Targeting: new unbound Ping Target key, as in BlindSlash. It pings your target with the game's own ping, whose sound comes from where the target stands, so with headphones you hear whether it is to your left or right, and it says how far away the target is ("25 yards"). Without a target you hear "No target". The ping is on Retail and WoW: Forever; in the other versions the key only says the distance.
* Environmental Alerts: new setting Repeat Delay (2 seconds by default, 0 to 30). At a shore, a doorway or a ledge the game switches between swimming and not, indoors and outdoors, or flying and not many times a second, and every switch was spoken: a few seconds at a shore gave half a minute of "swimming". Now each of these lines is said again only after the delay, and a line that is no longer true before it is spoken is dropped.
* Navigation: new module Name What I Bump Into. When Wall Detection says you are blocked, WowVision also says what you push against: the object in the way ("barrel", "fence", "tree"), outdoors the wall of the building ahead ("wall of Stormwind Keep"), indoors "wall". It knows Elwynn Forest with Stormwind and Dun Morogh with Ironforge on Classic Era, TBC Anniversary and WoW: Forever; Tirisfal Glades with the Undercity and the Deeprun Tram on TBC Anniversary and WoW: Forever; Teldrassil with Darnassus and the Swamp of Sorrows on TBC Anniversary; and Zephras Isle on WoW: Forever. Elsewhere nothing is said. It is on by default; switch it off with Enabled in Navigation, Name What I Bump Into.
* WowVision now says, a few seconds after you enter the world, when the game is not set to run custom scripts. WowVision's keys need that setting and do nothing without it, and until now nothing said why. It is missing when WowVision was installed before your first login to a game version, or by hand. The message says what to do: close the game, run the WowVision Updater, and start the game again. The installer (version 1.7) now also says when this step is left.
* Junk: you can now mark an item in the bags or bank as junk from its context menu (Shift+F10, Mark as Junk; Unmark as Junk takes the mark off). A marked item reads "[Junk]" before its name. With the new Merchant setting Automatically Sell Items Marked as Junk on (it is off by default), marked items are sold when a merchant opens, together with the grey items if Automatically Sell Poor Items is on. WowVision now says what it sold, naming the marked items; before, the sale of grey items was only printed in the chat. A favorite is never sold. The marks are kept for each character. There is also a Select Junk binding, without a key by default (UI, Bindings).
* Favorites: you can mark an item in the bags or bank as a favorite from its context menu (Mark as Favorite). A favorite reads "[Favorite]" before its name and is never sold at a merchant, even if it is also marked as junk or is grey. There is also a Select Favorite binding, without a key by default.
* Buffers: your buffs and debuffs without building an aura buffer. The new Speak Player Auras key (Alt+Ctrl+B) says every buff on you and then every debuff, each group with its number and each aura as the aura buffer says it (name, stacks, time left, dispel type); a group with none says so. The new Player Auras key (Alt+Shift+B) opens a short list of them: Tab moves between your buffs and your debuffs, Space reads an aura's tooltip with its description, Escape closes it. `/wv auras` opens the list, `/wv auras say` says everything, `/wv auras count` says how many there are. Both read through the aura buffer, so they work in combat and in instances on Retail and WoW: Forever, and they change none of your saved buffers.

### Modern
* WowNav (WoW: Forever): new setting Safe Routes (Percent Longer) in Navigation, WowNav Routes. A route may then be up to that many percent longer than the shortest way to keep away from the places where monsters and creatures usually stand; of those routes WowNav takes the one that leads the fewest yards near them. The summary at the start of a route, and the WowNav Route Status key, say how many yards the route leads near monsters compared with the shortest route. It is off (0) by default, and off, routes are planned exactly as before and just as fast. The navigation data is updated to its newest version (quest and gathering targets from Questie's database 1.1.5).
* WowNav (WoW: Forever): a route through a portal you walk into now leads you into the portal itself instead of ending in front of it. Between Rut'theran Village and Darnassus the route stopped a few yards before the portal, with no beacon into it; now the beacon leads in, the portal takes you to the other zone and the route goes on by itself.
* Quest colors: the color word of a quest (Grey, Green, Yellow, Orange, Red) in the quest log and in the quest tooltips of the scanner is now the one the game's quest log shows. It was worked out from the quest's level with a formula the game no longer uses for its log, which on WoW: Forever called a quest grey that the game shows green (a level 21 quest at level 27, for example).
* Group health: new Dispellable Debuffs settings. With Announce Dispellable Debuffs on (off by default), a watched group member who gets a debuff you can dispel is said with the debuff's type and the member's number, for example "Poison 3", and again every few seconds while the debuff stays on them. It has its own Watch Party and Watch Raid switches, a Repeat Every setting and Number First ("3 Poison"). It works in combat, and it looks at the members by itself about ten times a second, so it also works in instances where the game tells addons nothing about a changed debuff. If the game refuses the read, you hear "Dispellable debuffs unavailable here" once.
* Auction house (Mists of Pandaria Classic, Retail and Forever): fixed the Auctions tab failing with a "Duplicate control id" error and leaving you in your bags. It happened when the game listed two rows for one of your auctions, for example the part that has sold and the part that is still up.
* Spell Cooldowns monitor: a rule no longer says Ready (or On Cooldown) at every shot of a wand. A short lockout that something else puts on the spell is no longer taken for the spell's own cooldown: a cooldown of two seconds or less is ignored. Where the game hides the length of a cooldown, a cooldown that did not start with your own cast of the spell is said about two seconds after it began.
* Quest log: two headers with the same title no longer make the list fail with a "Duplicate control id" error. On WoW: Forever the log can list a city and a dungeon both as "Dalaran".
* Social window: friends for whom the game gives no BattleTag no longer make the Friends page fail with a "Duplicate control id" error.
* Group health: the silent number of a healthy group member is silent again. With some voices a short, low blip was heard in every repeat. With the Zira voice, numbers below 67 percent are no longer cut off at the end.
* Bags (Retail and WoW: Forever): new setting Show Item Lists (Windows, Containers; off by default). With it on, a new stop right after the bags, Item Lists, holds categories you open with Right and close with Left, like the scanner's: New Items (the items the game marks as new and everything that arrives in your bags, such as the reward of a quest, which the game did not mark on WoW: Forever), Junk and Favorites. New items stay listed until you have opened and closed the bags. The stop only exists while some category has something in it.

### Forever
* Character window: the Statistics tab now reads the value of each entry after its name. Money is spoken as gold, silver and copper, and a statistic the game has not recorded yet reads 0.
* LFG Forever: the group finder addon LFG Forever (by Artefius, opened with /lfg) is now read as a WowVision window when it is installed. It is a different thing from the game's own Looking for Group tool, which keeps its page; without the addon there is no key, no command and no module. WowVision opens its screen whenever LFG Forever's window opens and Escape closes it. The stops are the tabs (Filters, Whispers, Player ratings), the search (the Category and Activity menus, Search and Role check), the results, then the open tab's body (the four filter sections, which fold and unfold and say when a folded one holds changed filters, or the whisper log, or the recent party members with their stars) and Settings. A group's line reads its leader, what it is for, its members and their roles, the play style and the note; a player's line reads the name, level and class, roles, what for, the zone, the play style and the note; what the game keeps hidden in a dungeon, a raid or a fight is left out, never guessed. A line also says "guild member" or "friend", and your rating of the player (for a group, the lowest rating among its members), when LFG Forever marks it. Enter on a group is Ask to join (LFG Forever's own button, which whispers the leader the message you set there), on a player it is Invite, on your own listing it is Remove my listing; Backspace opens the game's menu for the listing and Shift+F10 lists the actions. In the Activity menu, the favorite star of a dungeon or raid is on the Right arrow of its row. New key Open or Close LFG Forever (Alt+Ctrl+G) and command /wv lfg. LFG Forever keeps its code private, so this reads it through the names it gives its window (written for version 2.3.0, checked against 2.5.0): if an update renames them, the part that depends on a name is left out and reported once, and if the whole window cannot be read you hear "LFG Forever's window has changed and cannot be read" once.
