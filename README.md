# WowVision

WowVision is a World of Warcraft addon for blind and visually impaired players. It makes the game playable without seeing the screen. It speaks through the text to speech voices built into the game and into Windows, so you do not need a screen reader running, although you can use one alongside it.

WowVision is still in active development, so expect some bugs.

The [WowVision manual](https://wow-vision.github.io/WowVision/) explains every feature, the keys you press and what you will hear. It is available in English and German:

## What it does

- Reads the game's windows and lets you use them with the keyboard, like a desktop application: bags, merchants, quest givers, talents, mail, the auction house and many more.
- Tells you what happens around you, such as a new target, entering combat, falling or running into a wall. Every alert can be spoken, played as a sound or turned off.
- Keeps information ready for you to read at any time, such as your health, gold, buffs and chat messages.
- Watches things for you, such as a debuff falling off your target or a spell being ready again.
- Guides you through the world with routes and sound beacons, a scanner for quest givers and objectives, and a minimap scan for herbs and ore.

## For developers

WowVision is also a framework for making the World of Warcraft UI accessible. It is modular, so features can be added or swapped out easily. The [Developer Guide](https://wow-vision.github.io/WowVision/developer/architecture.html) explains how it is built.
